How scoring works

Use the full 1–7 scale for every statement. Reverse-score items marked (R). For each axis, compute the average after reverse scoring; do not compare raw totals across axes. A higher average places you closer to the right-hand pole named in the axis title.

Interpret averages by axis: 1.0–2.4 strongly left-hand pole, 2.5–3.4 moderately left-hand pole, 3.5–4.4 mixed / intermediate, 4.5–5.4 moderately right-hand pole, 5.5–7.0 strongly right-hand pole.

How profile matching works

The matching engine turns each axis average into a five-step code: -2, -1, 0, +1, +2. It then computes both raw distance and a weighted distance against canonical profiles.

Double-weighted axes: F, E, A, P, O, Lp, Lc, H. Normal weight: C, Q, Adm, M, J, D.
